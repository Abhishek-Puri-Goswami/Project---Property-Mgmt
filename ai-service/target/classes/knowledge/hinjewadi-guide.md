# Hinjewadi Locality Guide

Hinjewadi is the largest and most prominent IT hub in Pune, home to Rajiv
Gandhi Infotech Park (Phases 1, 2, and 3), where major companies such as
Infosys, Wipro, TCS, and Cognizant operate large campuses.

**Why it suits IT professionals:**
- Very short commute (5-15 minutes) for anyone working within Hinjewadi's own
  IT parks.
- Dense cluster of mid-range and premium residential societies built
  specifically to serve the IT workforce.
- Good availability of co-working cafes, gyms, and IT-crowd-oriented
  restaurants and nightlife.

**Drawbacks:**
- Traffic congestion on the main Hinjewadi-Wakad road during peak hours is a
  well-known pain point.
- Public transport (metro) connectivity to central Pune is still expanding as
  of the latest phase extensions, so most residents rely on personal
  vehicles or company shuttles.

**Typical pricing:** 2 BHK apartments in Hinjewadi phases and adjoining
societies generally range from ₹65 lakh to ₹95 lakh depending on the exact
phase, builder reputation, and distance from the IT park gates.

**Verdict:** Hinjewadi is one of the most suitable localities in Pune for IT
professionals specifically because of its direct proximity to major tech
campuses, despite the traffic trade-off.
