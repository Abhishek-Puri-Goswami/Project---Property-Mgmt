# Pune Locality Guide

Pune is one of India's major IT and education hubs, located in Maharashtra. Its
real estate market is spread across several distinct zones, each with a
different character:

- **West Pune (Hinjewadi, Wakad, Baner, Aundh)** — dominated by the IT sector,
  home to Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park and major tech campuses. Best suited for
  IT professionals who want a short commute.
- **East Pune (Kharadi, Viman Nagar, Hadapsar)** — a mix of IT parks (EON,
  World Trade Center) and proximity to Pune Airport, popular with dual-income
  households.
- **Central Pune (Deccan, Camp, Shivajinagar)** — older, well-established
  areas with good connectivity, schools, and civic infrastructure, but higher
  prices per square foot.
- **South Pune (Kothrud, Warje, Sinhagad Road)** — residential-first
  neighborhoods with a quieter character, good for families.

Average 2 BHK apartment prices in Pune typically range from ₹55 lakh in the
outer suburbs to ₹1.2 crore or more in prime central or IT-corridor locations,
depending on project quality and distance from the nearest IT park or metro
corridor.
