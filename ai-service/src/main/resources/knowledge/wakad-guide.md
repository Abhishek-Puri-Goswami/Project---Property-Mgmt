# Wakad Locality Guide

Wakad sits immediately adjacent to Hinjewadi and has grown into a preferred
residential extension for people working in the Hinjewadi IT corridor who
want slightly better residential infrastructure than Hinjewadi itself offers.

**Why it suits IT professionals:**
- 10-20 minute commute to Hinjewadi Phase 1/2 IT parks.
- More mature residential infrastructure (schools, hospitals, malls like
  Xion Mall) compared to newer Hinjewadi phases.
- Well connected to the Mumbai-Pune Expressway and Pune-Bangalore Highway.

**Drawbacks:**
- The Hinjewadi-Wakad-Baner stretch shares the same traffic bottleneck
  during office hours.
- Property prices have risen significantly over the last several years due
  to high demand from the same IT workforce.

**Typical pricing:** 2 BHK apartments in Wakad typically range from ₹70 lakh
to ₹1 crore, generally slightly higher than comparable Hinjewadi properties
because of the more developed social infrastructure.

**Verdict:** Wakad is a strong alternative to Hinjewadi for IT professionals
who prioritize residential amenities and don't mind a marginally longer
commute.
