# Home Loan FAQ

**Q: How much of a property's value can a home loan typically cover?**
A: Most Indian banks and housing finance companies offer loan-to-value
ratios of 75-90% of the property value, depending on the loan amount slab;
the remainder must be paid as a down payment by the buyer.

**Q: What documents are needed to apply for a home loan?**
A: Identity and address proof, income proof (salary slips or ITRs),
bank statements, property documents, and a duly filled loan application
form. Self-employed applicants typically need additional business proof and
audited financials.

**Q: How is EMI calculated?**
A: EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1], where P is the principal loan
amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12), and n is the loan
tenure in months.

**Q: What factors affect the interest rate offered?**
A: Credit score (CIBIL), income stability, loan tenure, loan-to-value ratio,
and whether the loan is fixed or floating rate.

**Q: Can prepayment reduce the total interest paid?**
A: Yes. Making partial prepayments, especially early in the loan tenure,
significantly reduces the total interest paid over the life of the loan
since interest is calculated on the outstanding principal.
