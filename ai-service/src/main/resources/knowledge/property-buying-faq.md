# Property Buying FAQ

**Q: What is the typical process for buying a resale/ready-to-move property
in India?**
A: Shortlist properties, verify title and ownership documents, negotiate
price, sign an Agreement to Sell, pay stamp duty and register the Sale Deed
at the local sub-registrar office, then take possession.

**Q: What documents should a buyer always verify before purchasing?**
A: Title deed, encumbrance certificate, property tax receipts, occupancy
certificate (for ready properties), building plan approval, and (for
under-construction projects) RERA registration.

**Q: What is stamp duty and who pays it?**
A: Stamp duty is a state government tax on property transactions, paid by
the buyer at the time of registration. Rates vary by state; in Maharashtra
it is typically around 5-7% of the property value depending on the city and
buyer category.

**Q: Is it safe to buy an under-construction property?**
A: It can be safe if the project is RERA-registered, the builder has a
credible track record, and payments are linked to construction milestones
rather than paid upfront in full.

**Q: What is carpet area vs built-up area vs super built-up area?**
A: Carpet area is the actual usable floor area within the walls. Built-up
area adds wall thickness and balconies. Super built-up area additionally
includes a share of common areas like lobbies and staircases. RERA now
requires pricing to be quoted per carpet area.
