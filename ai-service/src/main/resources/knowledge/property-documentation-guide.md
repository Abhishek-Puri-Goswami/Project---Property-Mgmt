# Property Documentation Guide

Buying a property in India involves several important legal documents.
Understanding each one helps avoid disputes and fraud.

- **Title Deed** — proves legal ownership and the chain of previous owners.
  Always verify there are no gaps in the ownership chain.
- **Encumbrance Certificate** — confirms the property is free of any
  outstanding legal or financial liabilities (mortgages, loans).
- **RERA Registration Certificate** — mandatory for most new residential
  projects in India; confirms the project is registered with the state's
  Real Estate Regulatory Authority and provides recourse for delays.
- **Occupancy Certificate (OC)** — issued by local municipal authorities
  once a building is deemed fit for occupation; required before moving in.
- **Completion Certificate (CC)** — confirms the building was constructed
  according to the approved plan.
- **Sale Deed** — the final legal document that transfers ownership from
  seller to buyer, registered at the sub-registrar's office.
- **Property Tax Receipts** — proof that property taxes have been paid up to
  date by the current owner.

Buyers should insist on original documents (or certified copies) and, where
possible, have them reviewed by a property lawyer before making any advance
payment.
